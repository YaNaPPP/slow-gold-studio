I The gold

Why we only use gold that already exists

Gold doesn’t wear out. It doesn’t rust, fade or lose its worth. Almost every gram ever mined is still here, in vaults, in coins and in drawers: rings nobody wears, chains that broke years ago. We don’t dig for new gold. We give gold that already has a story a second life.

224,200t

All the gold mined in human history still exists. Melted together, it would make a cube about 22 metres wide.

44%

of it is jewellery, about 98,700 tonnes. A lot of it sits in drawers, waiting for someone to wear it again.

0g

III The making

Four weeks.
One pair of hands.

No moulds and no production line. One goldsmith takes your piece from the first sketch to the last polish, and you can follow every step.

Week 1Week 4
01 Week 1

We listen

Tell us whose gold it was and who will wear it next. We draw the piece around that story and send you a sketch with the final weight and price. Nothing is melted until you say yes.

Sketch 01
Wide band · 7.8 g for Mila, from Oma’s ring
02 Week 2

We melt

Your gold is weighed in front of you, or on video if you send it to us. Then it is melted at 1,064 °C, refined and cast into one small bar. Only your gold is in the crucible.

03 Weeks 2–3

We forge

The bar is rolled, bent and hammered into shape by hand. Every mark on the surface is a hammer strike, so no two pieces will ever look alike, not even two made from the same gold.

04 Week 4

We write it down

Your piece comes with its Gold Passport: the weight that went in, the weight that came out, where the gold came from and the story you told us. So it can be passed on again one day.

Questions

Before you decide

Is recycled gold as good as newly mined gold?

Yes. Gold is an element, so it does not wear out. Refining brings it back to the same purity as freshly mined gold, and every piece is hallmarked 585 (14k) or 750 (18k).

How do I get my gold to you?

Bring it to the atelier by appointment, or send it with insured shipping we arrange. We film the weighing and send you the video before anything is melted.

What if I don’t have enough gold?

We top it up with our recycled gold and tell you exactly how many grams came from you and how many from us. Both appear in your Gold Passport.

Can you keep the stones?

Yes. Stones are taken out before melting, cleaned and checked. We can set them in the new piece or return them to you.

How long does it take?

About four weeks from the approved sketch to the finished piece. We send photos at every step.

What if I don’t like the sketch?

We draw again. If we still can’t find the right design together, you pay nothing for the sketches and your gold comes back exactly as you sent it.
